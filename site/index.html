A calm menu bar app for macOS

Deep focus never costs you a meeting.

Today’s time and today’s events at the edge of your attention, in the macOS menu bar. Free and open source, with no telemetry, for Apple silicon Macs with macOS 27 or later.

Not released yet

How to get it

What it does

Time stays in view, quietly.

When you hyperfocus, time disappears. Leve keeps it in view without asking for attention. It has no window to manage.

One short line in the menu bar

It says “Free until 2:00 PM”, “in 12 min” or “20 min left”, never the event’s name, so it is safe on a shared screen.

  • Free until 2:00 PM
  • in 12 min
  • 20 min left

A notification first

A notification arrives a few minutes before each event.

A calm full screen

Just before an event, a calm alert covers every display, with a Join button, until you join or close it.

It says the time

Leve says the time on the half hour during the day, says the next event two minutes before it starts, and stays quiet during events.

  • It’s 10 o’clock
  • It’s 10:30
  • Standup in 2 minutes

Breaks, when you want them

Leve can ask for a break after a stretch of work. Take It Later leaves “Break due” beside the icon until you take it. A break never pauses your music on its own.

  • Skip This Break
  • Take It Later
  • Pause Music and Videos

Each calendar its own level

All alerts, No full screen, No alerts, or Hidden, for each calendar. Pause everything for 30 minutes, an hour, or until tomorrow.

Privacy

Your calendars stay on your Mac.

Leve reads today’s events from the calendars on this Mac and keeps its settings on this Mac. It never changes your calendars.

The only thing it sends over the network is the update check: it reads Leve’s update feed on GitHub, once a day or when you choose Check for Updates… in the menu. Nothing about you, your Mac or your calendars leaves it. You can turn automatic checks off in Settings.

Today only, by design: no tasks, no notes, no event editing, nothing about tomorrow until midnight.

Not released yet

There is no download yet.

Leve has no published release, so there is no disk image to download. The first release will be a signed and notarized disk image on GitHub, with a Homebrew cask, and Leve will update itself. It needs macOS 27 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Until then, build it from source with Xcode 27:

$git clone https://github.com/martonpaulo/leve.git && cd leve && make installCopied!
