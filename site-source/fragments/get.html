Leve has no published release, so there is no disk image to download. The first release will be a signed and notarized disk image on GitHub, with a Homebrew cask, and Leve will update itself. It needs macOS 27 or later on a Mac with Apple silicon.

Until then, build it from source with Xcode 27:
